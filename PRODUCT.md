# Yat Ricco Company — Product Brief

**Register:** brand (marketing site — the design IS the product)

## What it is
A conversion-focused brand + light-commerce website for **Yat Ricco Company**, a
wellness and lifestyle retailer in Samarahan, Sarawak, Malaysia. The owner,
Netti binti Sahari, sells BE wellness products and today operates mostly through
Facebook and manual WhatsApp chats. This site is the digital storefront that
makes the business look established, trustworthy, and easy to buy from.

## Audience
- Working professionals & business owners who want fast, convenient ordering.
- Parents & families looking for trusted wellness products and reliable support.
- Wellness enthusiasts & first-time buyers who want guidance before they buy.
- Core area: Samarahan + Kuching, Sarawak. Future: all of Malaysia.

## Promise
"Health & Wellness, Made Simple." A trusted wellness partner, not just a product
seller — helping people make informed lifestyle decisions with one-click
convenience and personal support.

## Must-have outcomes
- Look 10x more credible than a Facebook page.
- Make WhatsApp the primary, frictionless purchase + enquiry channel.
- Showcase product categories with clear "From RM" pricing.
- Capture consultation/booking enquiries.
- Multilingual surface (EN / Bahasa Melayu / 中文 / Iban) like peer KOBIS builds.
- Built-in helper chat + floating WhatsApp so no enquiry goes unanswered.
